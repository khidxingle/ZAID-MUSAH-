01 / About

A practice across art, culture and public life.

Zaid Musah is a Ghanaian emerging visual artist studying Painting and Sculpture at Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology (KNUST). His interests move between personal experience and wider social structures, asking how identity, memory, power and belonging are formed.

01 — ARTIST

Making meaning through material.

Painting, mixed media and material experimentation are ways to think through complexity, connection and inner chaos.

02 — LEADER

Culture as a public resource.

Interested in building spaces where young people can participate in cultural life and creative development.

03 — ENTREPRENEUR

Creative work in the world.

Building a body-art studio and learning how design, service and independent creative practice can support one another.

02 / Art practice

Looking for what sits beneath the surface.

My practice is interested in the unfinished, the contradictory and the things that remain difficult to name. I use visual form to investigate the relationship between inner experience and the systems surrounding us.

MATERIAL

Mixed media & painting

Exploring texture, layering, gesture and the tension between control and disorder.

QUESTIONS

Identity & institutions

Considering how cultural memory, public power and social expectations shape everyday life.

DIRECTION

Rhythm & unfinishedness

Developing a visual language where repetition, interruption and incompletion carry meaning.
